🚀 Quick 3-Step Setup for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

  1. 1
    Download & Extract the Extension ZIP

    Click the download button below to get darkshield-extension.zip and unzip it to a folder on your computer.

  2. 2
    Open Extensions Page & Enable Developer Mode

    Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions, brave://extensions) and toggle on "Developer mode" in the top-right corner.

  3. 3
    Click "Load unpacked" & Select Extracted Folder

    Choose the unzipped folder. DarkShield AI will immediately activate and protect your everyday shopping tabs!
